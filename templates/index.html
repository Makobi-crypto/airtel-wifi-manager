How Anti-Hotspot / Anti-Tethering Enforcement Works

When a smartphone or laptop connects to your Wi-Fi and turns on its personal hotspot, that device acts as a router. Packets from any secondary device connecting to that hotspot have their TTL (Time to Live) decremented from 64 to 63 (or 128 to 127). With Anti-Hotspot Guard ACTIVE, the system automatically detects and drops those packets at your gateway: the authorized device stays online, but any secondary phone, tablet, or PC connected to their personal hotspot is blocked from accessing the internet.
